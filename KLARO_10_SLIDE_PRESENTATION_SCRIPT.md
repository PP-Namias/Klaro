# Klaro — 10-Slide Presentation Script (Hackathon Recording)

This is a **script-only** file for your final presentation recording.

- Deck format: 10 slides
- Language: English (American English)
- Audience: Hackathon judges, technical evaluators, product/business reviewers
- Style: Confident, clear, technical but accessible, impact-driven
- Emojis: None
- Video target length: 2:45 to 3:00

---

## Slide-by-slide script with timing

### Slide 1 — Title (8–10 seconds)

**On-screen focus:** Product name + tagline

**Script:**
Klaro is an AI-powered health platform for the Philippines. We help patients and caregivers understand complex medical documents instantly, in plain language, and guide them to the right next care step.

---

### Slide 2 — The Problem (15–18 seconds)

**On-screen focus:** Problem bullets

**Script:**
Today, lab reports, prescriptions, and discharge summaries are often difficult for non-clinicians to interpret. Patients leave with uncertainty, caregivers struggle to explain medical terms, and critical care decisions are delayed because understanding comes too late.

---

### Slide 3 — The Solution (15–18 seconds)

**On-screen focus:** End-to-end value proposition

**Script:**
Klaro solves this by combining medical document understanding with care coordination. Users upload a record, OCR extracts the data, AI explains findings in plain language, and Klaro helps users continue into chat, nearby care discovery, and booking.

---

### Slide 4 — How It Works (18–22 seconds)

**On-screen focus:** 5-step patient flow

**Script:**
The workflow is simple. First, the patient uploads or scans a document. Second, OCR and extraction structure key values. Third, the LLM generates understandable interpretation with safety guidance. Fourth, the patient asks follow-up questions in context-aware chat. Fifth, Klaro guides the user to care options and booking.

---

### Slide 5 — Differentiation (14–16 seconds)

**On-screen focus:** Why Klaro is unique

**Script:**
What makes Klaro different is that it connects understanding to action. We do not stop at analysis. We provide a full path from medical record interpretation to care navigation, built for real Filipino patient and caregiver workflows.

---

### Slide 6 — MVP Features (16–20 seconds)

**On-screen focus:** Feature list

**Script:**
Our MVP includes document upload on web and mobile, OCR with fallback, plain-language AI interpretation, contextual chat, nearby facility discovery, and doctor booking with payment flow. These are integrated into one coherent product experience.

---

### Slide 7 — User Impact (14–16 seconds)

**On-screen focus:** Outcomes and value

**Script:**
The impact is practical and immediate: faster understanding of results, stronger patient confidence, less confusion for caregivers, and better continuity from document interpretation to timely healthcare action.

---

### Slide 8 — Technical Credibility (16–20 seconds)

**On-screen focus:** Stack summary + optional architecture image

**Script:**
Klaro is built on a modern monorepo architecture: Expo for mobile, Next.js for web, and a centralized typed tRPC backend in `packages/api`. We use PostgreSQL and object storage, plus OCR and LLM services with verification safeguards for reliability.

---

### Slide 9 — Hackathon Demo Scope (14–16 seconds)

**On-screen focus:** End-to-end path for judges

**Script:**
For this hackathon, we demonstrate a complete flow: scan, explain, chat, discover care, then book and pay. This proves not only technical feasibility, but also product readiness and a strong user-centered narrative.

---

### Slide 10 — Closing (10–12 seconds)

**On-screen focus:** Final statement

**Script:**
Klaro transforms confusing medical records into clear guidance and actionable care decisions. Our goal is to help patients and families move from uncertainty to confident next steps, faster and with greater trust.

---

## Recording cues for a strong 3-minute submission

- Speak at a steady pace: around 125 to 140 words per minute.
- Pause 0.3 to 0.5 seconds between slides for cleaner editing.
- Keep transitions direct: problem -> solution -> proof -> impact.
- Prioritize clarity over speed; avoid over-explaining technical details.
