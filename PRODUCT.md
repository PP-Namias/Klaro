# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Filipino patients, caregivers, and families who need to understand lab results, prescriptions, and discharge summaries. Many use mobile devices and want explanations in local dialects with clear next steps.

## Product Purpose

Klaro is an AI-assisted health companion that explains medical documents in plain language, supports local dialects, and connects users to nearby care and licensed doctors. Success looks like fast understanding, reduced anxiety, and confident action.

## Brand Personality

Approachable, trustworthy, helpful, Filipino-centric. The tone should feel calm and clear in a medical context, with empathy and confidence.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered layouts with low whitespace
- Busy, multicolor palettes that dilute focus
- Visual noise that makes medical content feel overwhelming

## Design Principles

- Clarity over jargon, always
- Trust first, reassure with calm hierarchy
- Guide to action, not just information
- Local language and cultural fit by default
- Reduce anxiety with clean, breathable layouts

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. Support reduced motion, strong color contrast, and large touch targets for mobile users.
